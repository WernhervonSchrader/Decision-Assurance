# Research modes

Choose the least expensive mode that can answer the request. Requested limits may lower these caps;
they can never increase a mode or server cap.

| Mode | Maximum search results | Maximum extractions | Use for | Result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Quick | 5 | 2 | a current, bounded research answer where an evidence handoff is not required | research answer |
| Verified | 10 | 5 | source checking, business research, or a Decision Assurance evidence bundle | evidence bundle |
| Deep | 20 | 10 | material decisions where independent sources or conflicts must be examined | evidence bundle with conflict analysis |

Use Deep when the request explicitly asks for contradiction analysis or when sources materially
disagree. Server policy may make every maximum stricter. Never retry merely to evade a cap.
